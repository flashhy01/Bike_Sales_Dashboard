# Bike_Sales_Dashboard
Data Cleaning, Visualization and Analysis
 Bike Sales Performance Dashboard (2011 - 2016)
 
  
  **Project Overview**:      
This project focuses on analyzing retail bike sales data spanning from 2011 to 2016 across multiple global regions. The objective of this analysis was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights, identifying key drivers of revenue, profitability, geographic market trends, and customer demographic preferences. 

The interactive dashboard was built entirely in Microsoft Excel using advanced Pivot Tables, calculated fields, and dynamic slicers to empower stakeholders to make data-driven decisions regarding inventory allocation and targeted marketing strategies.

**Data Engineering & Preparation**


To ensure data integrity and provide deeper layers of analysis, the raw dataset underwent a thorough data cleaning and transformation process in Excel:


 Duplicate Removal: Identified and permanently removed duplicate records from the dataset to prevent skewed metrics and ensure an accurate count of unique transactions.

 
 Data Cleansing: Standardized data types across columns, verified structural consistency, and resolved any missing values.

 
 Feature Engineering (Age Demographics): 
 Created a nested logical function (`IF` statements) to group individual customer ages into standard business brackets:
 
   Youth (<25)
   
   Young Adults (25-34)
   
   Adults (35-64)
   
   Seniors (64+)
   
 Data Modeling:
 Built multiple interconnected Pivot Tables to aggregate millions in revenue and profit across years, countries, product categories, and gender lines.


 **Key Insights & Analysis**

 1. Financial Trends & Market Trajectory
 The Growth Era: Revenue and profit experienced a massive upward surge starting in 2012, peaking at an all-time high in 2015. 
 The 2016 Decline: There is a distinct dip in performance in 2016 across both revenue and profitability. While 2016 remains the second-highest grossing year in the dataset, this sharp trend reversal indicates a market contraction or sudden operational cost increases that require deep-dive auditing.

 3. Customer Demographics
 The Revenue Engine: The Adults (35-64) age group is overwhelmingly the dominant consumer segment, responsible for the vast majority of total revenue. Young Adults (25-34) represent the second-largest customer block.
 The Senior Gap: The Seniors (64+) demographic accounts for a negligible fraction of sales, showing either a complete misalignment in product offering or a total lack of targeted marketing to older populations.

 4. Geographic Performance
 The Core Markets: The United States stands out as the single largest revenue-generating market globally, followed steadily by Australia. 
 European Disparity: European markets (United Kingdom, Germany, and France) alongside Canada showcase uniform, lower-tier revenue plates. These regions exhibit steady but stagnant sales, pointing to higher localized competition or lower market penetration.


**Strategic Business Recommendations**

Based on the dashboard insights, the following data-backed recommendations are proposed for senior leadership:

1. Targeted Ad Spend Realignment: Allocate 60-70% of the digital marketing budget toward the Adult (35-64) demographic in the United States and Australia, focusing heavily on the specific product lines driving their high-volume orders.

2. Investigate the 2016 Margin Compression: Initiate an operational review into 2016's performance to determine whether the decline was caused by shifting customer acquisition costs, supply chain overhead, or a reduction in global unit orders.

3. Product Expansion vs. Market Penetration: Given the stagnation in European regions, run localized promotional offers or partner with regional cycling influencers to kickstart growth in lagging territories like France and Germany.


**Tech Stack Used**

 Software: Microsoft Excel
 
 Features: Data Cleansing (Remove Duplicates), Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Data Grouping, Conditional Formatting, and Advanced Formulae (`IF`, `SUM`, `AVERAGE`).
